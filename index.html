About

Hi, I’m Shifa Ashad, a creative designer✦

I design the things people judge a business by: the logo, the website, the feed, the box and the book cover. I believe most brands don't have a design problem. They have a consistency problem, and I fix it one touchpoint at a time.

I hold a BFA and an MFA in Visual Design from The University of Texas, where I learned how to make things look good. Years in in-house creative lead roles taught me how to make them work, on a phone screen, on a shelf and on a billboard.

I started as the designer, became the one who led the designers, and eventually owned how whole companies looked to the world. Now I work directly with founders, authors and marketing teams, without the agency layers.

My work moves between strategy and craft: brand systems and Shopify stores, campaign launches and picture books, dielines and design systems. The common thread is a simple test. Cover the logo, and you should still know whose work it is.

I notice the fonts on restaurant menus. It's a condition. It's also why my clients' menus look better.

Three things I won't skip

01The cover-the-logo test

Hide the name and you should still know whose work it is. That's the bar for every brand.

02Designed at real size

Thumbnail, shelf, phone in a dark room. Work gets checked where it will actually live.

03One designer, start to finish

The person you brief is the person who designs it. No hand-offs, no lost details.
